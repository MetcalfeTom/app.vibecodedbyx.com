# Fantasy Realm 3D — notes

## log
- v1.0 (2026-09-19): built per chat ("a separate 3D Fantasy Realm prototype with an explorable overworld, hero, and one enemy encounter"). Separate app, no code shared with the turn-based Fantasy Realm. Pure `<script id="eng">` (dual-exported): seeded 48×48 world — fbm value-noise terrain flattened on the start pad and in the Knight's clearing, ~150 trees scattered off the pad/clearing/a winding corridor (z = 2·sin(x/6)) and ≥1.6 apart; hero moves 6 u/s (dodge burst 12 u/s for 0.25 s, 0.5 s i-frames, 1.2 s cooldown), circle collision with trunks + bounds, dt clamped to 100 ms; ONE encounter — the Cursed Knight (60 hp) notices you within 9 units, closes at 4.2 u/s, winds up for 0.4 s then hits for 8 every 1.5 s in reach 1.9, leashed 7 units from his clearing, drops aggro past 11 and walks home; your strike does 8 in reach 2.2 with a 0.6 s cooldown; won/lost states freeze the run. Rendering = three.js r128 from cdnjs (the only network use): vertex-coloured flat terrain with a lighter corridor, instanced cone trees, dodecahedron stones ringing the clearing, primitive hero/knight figures (sword swing, halo flashes on hit, wind-up raises the sword), third-person camera behind the hero with yaw smoothing, hemisphere + directional light, fog. Controls: WASD/arrows camera-relative, Space strike, Shift dodge; touch stick + two buttons (hidden on fine-pointer desktops); HUD bars, aria-live state line, won/lost overlay with focus on "new forest". Seam `__F3` (freeze/step/setHero/draw). Tests: eng 31/31 node (seeded world, tree-free pad/clearing/corridor, corridor walk reaches the clearing, speed, tree push-out, bounds, dt clamp, aware/close/wind-up/blow timing, dodge i-frames, strike + cooldown + out-of-reach, leash + return, win, frozen after win, lose), probe 27/27 ×3 widths (WebGL frame painted — mid-height band sampled, camera follows, keys + arrows, touch controls ≥44 px on narrow screens, encounter via seam, strike/cooldown/wind-up sword pose/dodge/touch strike, win overlay + focus + restart, lose overlay, computed-style overlay check, stamp gutter), screenshots 1200/390.

## issues
- The `[hidden]` vs own-display trap struck again: `.overlay{display:grid}` kept the end overlay visible (and dimmed the whole scene) while every attribute check passed — the screenshot caught it; the probe now asserts computed display. Rule: any element with its own display rule gets an explicit `[hidden]{display:none}`.
- Fixture lessons: a walk test from the start pad runs into trees — test speed in the tree-free clearing; a dodge ROLLS the hero out of the Knight's reach, so an i-frame test must hold position; the 100 ms dt clamp means "wait 0.7 s" is seven steps, not one; read WebGL pixels from a mid-height band, a corner is often one flat colour.
