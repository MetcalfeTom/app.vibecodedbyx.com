      Dossier 2026-1005 · Issoire, Puy-de-Dôme

      The bronze cover is gone.

      In the night of 4 to 5 October 2026, the bronze cover Salvador Dalí sculpted for the book L'Apocalypse de Saint Jean was stolen from a contemporary art centre in Issoire, France.

      • The cover alone weighs about 150 kg, set with gold, precious stones and seashells.
      • The whole book: 210 kg, 150 m long unfolded, 150 parchments, 95,000 hand-written letters.
      • Seven painters (Dalí, Foujita, Georges Mathieu…) and seven writers (Cocteau, Jean Rostand, Cioran, Giono, Ernst Jünger…) worked on it for publisher Joseph Forêt, from 1958.
      • Nobody can explain how something so heavy left unnoticed.

      There is no picture of it here. So help the investigators: witnesses each remember a few details. Rebuild the cover on the empty plinth to match, and the curator will tell you if it fits the testimony.

      Facts: news reports, 6 Oct 2026. The witnesses in the game are made up, and what you build is your own homage, not the real cover.
