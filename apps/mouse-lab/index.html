What the numbers mean

Drift up/down adds up every vertical move while you rock sideways, in degrees of view (0.12° per pixel) and pixels. Level means within 3°, up to 10° is a slight drift, more is noticeable. A steady hand lands near zero; a browser or driver that adds its own vertical movement makes the view creep one way, run after run.

Lean splits that drift by direction: if moving right pushes the view up and moving left pushes it up too, it isn't your hand.

Spikes are single reports far bigger than your usual move: the view jumps somewhere you never pointed. Stalls are gaps of 60 ms or more with no reports while you keep moving.

Pointer lock, raw asks the browser to skip your system's mouse acceleration. Pointer lock isn't available on phones and some browsers.
