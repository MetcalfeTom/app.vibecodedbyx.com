THE CRATE

green rows mix harmonically with the playing deck: same Camelot slot, ±1 on the wheel, or the relative major/minor — and within ±6% BPM. ⇄ sync matches a deck's tempo and snaps its bars to the other deck; ◂ ▸ nudge the phase. Every record runs 32 bars (intro · build · main · break · drop · outro) — tap the strip to jump, tap an empty cue pad to mark the bar you're on, tap it again to drop there on the next beat, hold it to clear. Auto-mix beat-matches the closest green record in at the outro, starting from its cue 1.

every record is synthesized live in your browser — no samples, nothing copyrighted, nothing uploaded
