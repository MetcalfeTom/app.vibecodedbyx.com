    how scoring works here A word scores its letter values (Scrabble values, unless you change them under scoring). That gets multiplied by 1, plus one for each spicy letter in it and each tile it spends to zero, plus two for a pangram and two more when that pangram is your first word. So the word that empties three tiles at once is worth ×4, which is why the solver saves big finishes for the end. Every word needs the golden letter, words are 3 letters or longer, and each plain tile has 5 uses. A pangram (a word with all nine letters) as your first word makes the game Perfect if you also use up every tile with no hints: the whole score doubles. Pangrams sit on top until you play your first word. The game ends when every plain tile is used up, so the last word has to use exactly what is left. "then +N more" is the best finish the solver found after that word, without spicy bonuses and without repeating a word ("final" is the whole game's score that way, Perfect included); "dead end" means the tiles left over can't be used up with the words in your list.
