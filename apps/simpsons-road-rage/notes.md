- 2026-10-13 15:31 UTC — **v2.5 physics** (fela: "physics should be more realistic", helper session):
  - **engine**: pull-limited off the line (24, turbo 34), power-limited higher up (`ENGINE_POWER` 650, turbo sized for 74), rolling resistance 1.2 (+2.5 on grass) and air drag (`DRAG` solved so power = drag at 60). 0-30 in ~1.4 s, 55 at ~4.8 s, tops out at ~59.5. Coasting adds 1.5 engine braking; brakes and drag stop the car, never reverse it.
  - **velocity is the truth now**: `car.speed` is read off `velocity · forward` after each step (anything that wants to slow the car scales `car.velocity`, as the bus hit now does).
  - **tyres**: the nose follows the front wheels (`yaw = v·tan(steerAngle)/WHEELBASE`, eased at 10/s, capped at 1.3×grip/v); the tyres remove sideways slip in proportion to the slip angle (peak at `SLIP_PEAK` 0.12 rad) up to `GRIP` 50 (×0.85 on grass), past that `car.sliding` and they scrub speed. A grip term lines the car up with its velocity (2.5/s). Steering lock eases 0.62 → 0.15 rad with speed; keys ease in over ~0.1 s (`steerSm`).
  - **brake-drift** (brake + steer above 8): rear grip ×0.42, brake only 7/s, yaw cap ×1.25 → ~35° slide, about 0.9 rad of turn in 0.6 s, keeps ~29 of 40; let go and it gathers itself within a second.
  - **weight transfer** (looks only): damped springs (k 150, c 11) for pitch (nose dips braking, lifts on the gas) and roll (leans out of turns) driven by the measured acceleration, plus heave; the body pivots about a point 0.7 up. **Kerbs**: each wheel samples `groundAt`, the car tilts (`group.rotation.order = "YXZ"`) and the body squats then rebounds.
  - **walls**: bounce by how square the hit is (0.12 + 0.2×square, only above 6), Coulomb friction along the wall from the impact (0.3×(j−2) + a little while leaning on it), a yaw twist from a nose/tail hit, `wallT` 0.25 s of strong line-up so a graze turns the car along the wall, spring jolts, and **sparks** from the contact point (`fx.sparks`, 1 draw call).

## issues
- probes teleport the car with `placeCar(x, z, heading, speed)` (resets the drive state, springs and gear); never set `car.mesh.position` directly any more: the simulation lives in `car.pos`/`car.heading`/`car.drv` and the mesh is only drawn from them.
- the HUD speed is mph now (game speed × 2.237); `car.speed` is still metres per second.
- `populateDonuts()` runs at top level before later `const`s exist → the donut kit is a lazily built `var`. Keep new top-level consts above their first use.
- Building positions/sizes use Math.random (new town every load), so narrow car-width gaps between houses differ per load. Probes must not assume a layout.
- Top-level call order matters (TDZ): `buildLandmarks`, `populateRods`, `spawnTraffic` run at load, so their helpers are function declarations and `LANE`/`ROD_COUNT` sit near the top. `var donutKit = null` further down resets the kit after the first populate (harmless, rebuilt lazily).
- A car parked against a bus keeps that bus stopped (by design: buses stop on contact); traffic behind it queues.
- Headless probe: scratch `gaunt/mkrr.sh` swaps the supabase import for a local stub (`sbstub.js`) so tests never write to the real table.

- donut jump: crossing the ramp side-on at speed is a real kicker (16 m/s → a ~5 m hop that lands on its wheels; heading at the donut shop it bonks the shop wall mid-air, collisions are 2D). Gentler skirts would need much wider wedges.
- donut jump probes: random donuts can lie on the run-up lane (pickDonutSpot ignores keepClear), so a jump run may also eat a regular donut (+2 s). The statue's `st.donut` stays set after pickup; check `.parent`.
