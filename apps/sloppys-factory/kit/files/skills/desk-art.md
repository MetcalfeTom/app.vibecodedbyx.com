# Drawing a piece for one person

Sloppy's own write-up. Viewers who reach a level on stream get a piece of pixel art in the little room behind Sloppy: a trinket on the desk or a picture in a frame. The same method works for any tiny custom sprite.

## Sizes
| Size | What it is | What fits |
|---|---|---|
| 8x8 | a trinket on a desk | one object or one face; two ideas merge into one shape |
| 12x12 | a bigger desk thing | a character with one prop, side or three-quarter view |
| 16x20 | a framed picture | a portrait: face 6 px wide, a bold hat or hair, a simple background |
| 36x24 | a big framed picture | a scene: 2-3 sky bands, horizon low, one warm focal light |

## The flow
1. Ask what they want, in their words.
2. Draw three drafts that differ in the idea (pose, framing or colours), not three shades of one.
3. Show all three side by side, big and at the size people will really see them. Judge by the small copy.
4. They pick one or ask for changes. Change a few pixels yourself; redo bigger changes as a new round.
5. Put the chosen one up and tell them it's there.

## Drawing
- One idea that reads at this size. The thing they asked for is the biggest, brightest part, and the features that say what it is (a frog's eyes, a cat's ears, a dragon's horns) sit on the outline where nothing covers them.
- Type the grid row by row, one letter per colour, in a small script that asserts every row's length and writes the piece as JSON.
- Flat shape first, then a 1 px dark outline round it (a helper can add the ring round everything that isn't empty), then light from the top left: two or three pixels of highlight and shadow, never a ring of shading.
- Keep big areas flat. Texture is two or three deliberate marks, not a pattern.
- Render, look, fix. Two to five rounds: delete stray pixels, even out stair-steps, make sure nothing important got covered.

## Colours on a dark wall
The pieces hang on a dark wall, so fill with middle and light shades and keep the darkest ones for outlines, shadows and backgrounds. A dark fill disappears on stream.

## The trap
A grey cat asleep on a silver laptop vanished into one grey blob. Things that touch need to differ in brightness as well as colour, with an outline between them: a light cat on a dark laptop with a glowing screen reads at once.
