    ⚠ Floor rule: the sandbox

    The whole floor sits in a sandbox. Routine work (edit a file, run a test) goes ahead without anyone clicking "allow", while a safety check still blocks risky actions. That's how the factory runs on its own: autonomy = a sandbox + guardrails + an undo button (git), not "approve everything".
