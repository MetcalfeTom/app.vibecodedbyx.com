VAULTGRID

backup inventory & disaster terminal — the simulator sibling of The Backup Rack. Everything simulated, everything stays in this browser.

3

three copies

the original on your machine plus two independent copies — independent meaning no shared disk, no shared roof-and-cable fate.

2

two kinds of media

spread the copies across different storage — SSD, HDD, cloud — so one technology's bad batch can't take both.

1

one offsite & offline

at least one copy lives outside the building, and at least one sits unplugged or cold — beyond fires and beyond ransomware's cables.
