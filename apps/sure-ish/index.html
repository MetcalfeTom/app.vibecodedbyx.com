sure-ish

an uncertainty-aware interface. four instruments measure genuinely noisy things — your device's clocks, your own hands — and none of them will show you a bare number. every reading carries its doubt: a confidence interval, a sample count, and a blur that only sharpens as the evidence comes in.

  • rule 1 never a bare number
  • rule 2 n is always visible
  • rule 3 no data ≠ zero
  • rule 4 blur is honest — sharpness is earned
  • rule 5 actions unlock on confidence, not clicks
