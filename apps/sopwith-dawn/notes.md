# Sopwith Dawn

A fancy remake of the 1984 Sopwith, asked for by varj1 on 2026-10-03: "make a fancy version of the classic Sopwith game",
"make sure the colors are in the classic CGA scheme", "the looks", "factories", "crappy internal pc speaker sound", "fuel tanks".

## Art direction
- Canvas only ever uses CGA palette 1 high intensity: black #000, cyan #55ffff, magenta #ff55ff, white #fff. Shades come from 4x4 Bayer dithering, never from other colours (text on the canvas is snapped to one colour + black rim; rotated sprites use nearest sampling).
- 200 px tall like the CGA screen, 300-400 px wide depending on the window, scaled up pixelated.
- Sky: plain black (v1.1, varj1). A magenta far ridge (parallax .2), a black near ridge with a cyan rim (.45), white/cyan clouds (.55).
- Player: white/cyan biplane; enemies: magenta with a black and white cross. Ground: white edge, cyan grass, magenta earth dithering to black.
- Water (v1.35 regatta): an opaque C/K Bayer fill (11/16 black) with a lighter band (5/16) just under a cyan surface line and slow white glints; boats are drawn in boat coordinates and squeezed by cos(spin) when they turn, so a turn reads as the hull foreshortening, with the mast always upright.
- Thunderhead (v1.36): one 1300 x 200 prerender (plus a flash-lit copy, +.28 on every value) placed with its local x 320 at the front: a thin anvil overhang 320 px ahead (underside thunAB), cauliflower disks along the top with bright rims, a banded dark shelf cloud at the base, and a black rain shaft (Bayer fade-in, 8 px bands of different darkness, slanting cyan streaks from a lookup table). Drawn after MID and before the terrain, so the hills cut it; the decorative CLOUDS are skipped. Hail (white dots) at the leading edge and lying on the ground behind it, cyan rain behind that, bolts as px lines (2 px wide while fresh). Balloons: 19 x 21 envelopes (stripes, bands, harlequin in W/C), a magenta basket, heads over the rim, a trail rope streaming away from the wind.
- Pink balloon (v1.37): escEnv() is a 25 x 31 envelope, magenta with dithered shading, white gore seams, a little crown on top and a chequered C/W girdle over the basket; the Baron is two pixels of head plus a magenta scarf and a fist that shakes for 1.4 s after each throw (B.fist). Loot sprites in escItem(): urn, honey pot, barometer dial, cider barrel, Gerald's hat (sways as it falls), the cup under a little parasol. Falling loot gets a blinking ground marker; off-screen things get a CATCH / BARON arrow at the screen edge.
- The Baron's side (v1.39): autumn trees shedding M/C leaves; a striped border barrier with a hut and his pink B flag; the castle on a flat crag (terrain is clamped to y >= 132, so a crag can't go higher: the towers carry the height) with a dithered white hall, pink roof, lit windows, two towers with battlements and a searchlight on each (hngBeam, the same dithered cone as the old searchlights). The balloon hangar is a pink barrel roof on white ribs with a B, a door slab at each end (cyan with white bands) and a winch with a crewman beside each. Inside, dark dust and a dithered outline of something big until you've been in; then the airship is drawn lit (hngAirship: a 125 x 31 pink cigar with white seams, fins, a big white B and a gondola, hung on cables).
- Sound: one square-wave oscillator like the PC speaker; one sound at a time, highest priority wins (win tune > boom > moo/hit > gun/landing > bomb whistle > engine buzz that follows speed).
