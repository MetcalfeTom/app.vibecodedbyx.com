Drag to wander, pinch or scroll to zoom. Each dot gets a grey ring for the blast that knocks houses down and a soft orange one for the heat that burns skin.

A thought experiment, not a forecast: no real targets, just even spacing. Warhead counts are rounded public estimates (2025 inventories, and the 1986 peak). Blast and burn sizes are rough, scaled from Hiroshima (blast with the cube root of the yield, burns a little faster). Real effects depend on burst height, terrain and weather, and fallout drifts far beyond both rings.
