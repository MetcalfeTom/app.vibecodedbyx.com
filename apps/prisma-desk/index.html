6 · What this desk is not

honest limits: a workbench for learning and small projects — not a substitute for RevMan, metafor, or a statistician. Continuous two-group outcomes only; the DerSimonian–Laird estimator is the classic default but not the last word (REML usually behaves better); no risk-of-bias tooling, no funnel plots, no publication-bias tests. Every number on this page is derived from your table and can be checked by hand against the formulas printed above — that is the one guarantee.

7 · The real toolbox — with fit and poor fit

names only, no links — your search bar is fresher than any URL. each card says how it plugs into this desk safely, and just as honestly, when to skip it.

Zotero stage · collect

free, open-source reference manager — one-click capture, PDFs, and clean exports.

safe use: export your collection as RIS and paste it into section 1 — records land locally, nothing leaves this page.

poor fit: a ten-paper class assignment. a folder and honesty may be all the infrastructure you need.

OpenAlex stage · collect

open bibliographic dataset of the world’s papers — identifiers, metadata, and citations, free and scriptable.

safe use: the reproducible backbone: auditable search queries and metadata enrichment your protocol can cite exactly.

poor fit: point-and-click reviewing — it is a dataset with an interface, more pipeline than reading room.

Rayyan stage · screen

web platform for blinded dual screening at scale — the grown-up version of section 2's rater buttons.

safe use: keep decisions exportable and mirror the final calls back here, so the PRISMA flow stays derived from one source.

poor fit: solo reviews — blinding yourself is just mood lighting. two raters or don't bother.

ASReview stage · screen

open-source active-learning screener — a model ranks the pile so you read the likely-relevant abstracts first.

safe use: the machine orders, the human decides — every include is still yours, mirrored back into this flow, and your stopping rule belongs in the methods section.

poor fit: small reviews — training a ranker on forty abstracts is a hat on a hat. just read them.

Covidence stage · screen

review-management platform — screening, extraction, and risk-of-bias workflows for whole teams, Cochrane-partnered.

safe use: this desk’s entire flow at team scale; keep the exports so the audit trail outlives the subscription.

poor fit: small unfunded projects — the subscription outweighs a modest review’s needs. this free desk exists for a reason.

DistillerSR stage · screen

enterprise systematic-review platform with deep audit trails, built for regulatory-grade evidence work.

safe use: when compliance genuinely demands full traceability of every decision — that is its home turf.

poor fit: learning, students, or small teams — enterprise machinery for enterprise problems, nothing more.

ResearchRabbit stage · map

citation-graph explorer — seed papers grow into the neighborhood your search string missed.

safe use: treat every discovery as a candidate: it enters at "identified" and faces the same screening as everything else. no side doors into the flow.

poor fit: protocols that demand a reproducible search string — graph wandering is wonderful and unauditable.

Semantic Scholar stage · map

free AI-enriched academic search and citation graph — paper summaries, citation contexts, an open corpus.

safe use: a discovery lens with an open API: reproducible pulls are possible, and every find still enters at “identified.”

poor fit: letting one engine define your search — field coverage varies, and medicine’s dedicated indexes still set the standard.

The Lens stage · map

free scholarly and patent search built for citation chaining — walk a paper’s family tree in both directions.

safe use: backward = mine an included study’s reference list for what it built on; forward = follow who cited it since, to where the idea went. log both as “citation searching” in your methods, and every find still enters at “identified.”

poor fit: as your only search — chaining follows the crowd, and brand-new or isolated work has no citation trail to walk yet.

Consensus stage · map

AI-assisted search over the research literature — ask a question, get candidate papers and a sense of where findings lean.

safe use: a discovery lens only. every paper it surfaces enters at "identified" and faces the same screen as the rest — and every claim gets verified in the paper itself, never in the summary.

poor fit: citing. an AI synthesis is a starting point, not a source — if it ends up in your bibliography, something went wrong.

GRADEpro stage · grade

the GRADE working group’s tool for rating certainty of evidence and building summary-of-findings tables.

safe use: after the pooling: rate each outcome’s certainty and say out loud how sure the diamond deserves to feel.

poor fit: before outcomes exist — grading needs evidence to grade. it is the last chapter, not the first.

Obsidian stage · write

local-first markdown knowledge base — notes that link, a graph that thinks, files that stay yours.

safe use: archive this desk's JSON export alongside your notes for provenance — the extraction table travels with the argument.

poor fit: a team's shared live extraction table — a spreadsheet (or this desk's export, passed around) collaborates better than anyone's personal vault.
