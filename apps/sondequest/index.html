SONDEQUEST

Twice an hour (game time), a weather balloon goes up carrying a radiosonde. It climbs, drifts with the winds, bursts, and parachutes down. Your job: read its telemetry, chase the shrinking landing ellipse, and get there before rival hunters plant their flag. Roads are fast, forests are slow, lakes are a mistake.
