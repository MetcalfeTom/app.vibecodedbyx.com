# Musical Parade

Build a marching band one player at a time; each instrument adds its part to an 8-bar march in B-flat while the band high-steps down a festival street.

## 3D art direction (parade3d.js)
- Look: clay-lit people with real proportions (angienimo, v1.8: "realistic 3d humans, including the crowd, not toys"): lathe torsos/limbs, knees, shoes, hands with thumbs, faces (eyes, brows, lips), natural hair; props stay low-poly vertex-coloured primitives; brass/silver on a separate metal material with a per-band PMREM sky env map so horns flash.
- Mood: a moving sun (v1.9). While the band plays, a day passes in 5 minutes (DAYH = 24/300 h/s): DAY keyframes by hour 4.5..28.5 (sky top/horizon, hemi, sun colour/intensity, elevation, night 0..1), smoothstepped. Each day band keeps its signature light at its HOME hour (march 15:00, carnival 12:30, jazz 18:18) by lerping toward LIGHT/STY within 2.2 h. Sun from 5:24 to 20:12 (arc from left to right, always a little in front so faces keep light), then a moon (moonTex, smaller disc). Night: lit windows (winM warm), brighter lamps + additive lamp pools on the far pavement (LPOOL), stars, dark far skyline, dimmer clouds. Sky = one 4x256 canvas redrawn when the hour moves 0.03 h; brass env map re-made every 1.5 h. neon = always night (magenta laser-grid road with emissive map, glowing signs/trims): no cycle, no clock button. The 🌅 clock pill (3D only) shows the street time and skips ahead 3 h.
- Camera: 3/4 view from the near sidewalk, fits the band width; phones (aspect<1) swing to yaw .82 so the band comes toward you. Drag = orbit (touch: horizontal only, pan-y scrolls the page). Wheel / pinch = zoom 0.28-1.6 (zoomBy returns false at a limit so the page scrolls on); zoom resets on a style change.
- World layers: road + sidewalks (static meshes, textures scroll), recycled building slots in a `world` group (position.x = -(u - base*BW)), crowd on the far sidewalk, far skyline boxes at z -34..-42, sun plane, clouds/stars.
- Budget (headless, 8 marchers + full crowd): 86-100 draw calls, 185-250k tris incl. shadow pass (v1.8). The crowd is instanced parts (legs, torso, arms, hands, head, 2 hair shapes, cap, hand flags) frustum-culled per building slot and packed into the first j instances every frame; colours cached per person in CCOL at setWorld. Adaptive: avg frame > 24 ms over frames 20-90 -> pixel ratio 1, shadows off.

All sound synthesized with WebAudio (no samples): brass = saw+square through an enveloped lowpass, piccolo = triangle with vibrato, bells = 3 inharmonic sines, drums = filtered noise + pitched sine. A second player of the same instrument plays a harmony line (third below / fifth / octave), more add volume. New players join on the next beat. Legs step on the beat from the audio clock, the street scrolls with the song position, and the crowd raises arms as the band grows. Full band = confetti + cheer. ## issues
- The clock falls back to performance.now() until the AudioContext is running ('p' mode), then re-anchors to the audio clock ('a' mode).
- Headless test copy: SP/parade/mkt.sh + probe.js (modes: default click-through test, audio=1 offline render (+style=, from=), og=1 og screenshot, idle=1, shot=<letters> screenshot, sw=1 switch bands mid-parade).
- Per-band synth voices: pl(k) picks PLAYN for neon, else PLAY. Neon buildings are 0.62x tall and the sun rises with VH so it shows on phones.
- Songs live in EV, rebuilt by buildSong(style); LOOP is 128 (march, carnival) or 256 (jazz). sched() counts steps from st.s0 and adds swing offsets (SWG) for jazz.
- Realistic look: scene() = back() (drawn into BGC at 0.5x when st.real) + street(); castShadow() reuses marcher() on SHX; grade() runs after the marchers. marcher() checks st.real for proportions and gradients.
- 3D: index.html keeps the song, band list and clock; parade3d.js only draws. frame() calls R3.render(p,now,exc) then draw(), which in 3D just clears the 2D canvas (it sits on top, transparent) and draws notes/confetti/hint. pick(e) -> R3.pick(clientX,clientY). Probe p3d=1 tests pick/tap/hold/drag in 3D and prints draw calls; shot=...&look=3d screenshots (headless SwiftShader is slow, so the 'Recruit your band' hint fades late in shots).
- Solos: st.solo={id,k,from,to} in song steps; sgain(k,S) sets OUTK before each pl(k) call and out() inserts a gain node when OUTK!==1. Probe hold=1 tests hold/tap/auto-end; shot=...&solo=<letter> screenshots a solo.
