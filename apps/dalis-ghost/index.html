Soufflé of Startled Detective, Port Lligat style

  • 1 detective, slightly startled
  • 3 soft watches, left in the sun until runny
  • a pinch of ants (they find their own way in)
  • 1 crutch, to hold the soufflé up
  1. Wait for the light to go golden, then a little longer.
  2. Fold the detective gently into the warm sand.
  3. Pour the melted hours over everything.
  4. Serve on a plate in the middle of the desert. Curl the moustache upwards.

— the Ghost
