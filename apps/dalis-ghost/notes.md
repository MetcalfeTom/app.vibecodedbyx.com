# Dalí's Ghost (for marcipopsis / Marci)

Marci's idea (2026-10-07): a sequel to The Mystery of Dalí's Apocalypse (apps/empty-plinth): Dalí's ghost stole the cover himself. A phone game playable without instructions; "a thing to solve about Dalí's life and work in each field". Then the bigger vision: walk through a 3D museum (light 3D is ok), at least 6 rooms, each room a part of his life, each with its own surreal vibe based on an artwork, find all the pieces. Losing ending: you get cooked with a recipe from the cookbook (after 10 wrong answers or rooms not finished in time) - write our own surreal recipe, don't copy the book.

Marci's rooms so far: Madrid fine-arts education (expelled from the academy, 1926); Impressionism + Renaissance masters -> Cubism and the avant-garde; The Persistence of Memory (finished August 1931); the dead older brother, also named Salvador ("two drops of water, different reflections"); Alexandre Dumas (Marci: second room, and he's in the cookbook - VERIFY before stating as fact); the cookbook dinner.

## art direction (v2, 3D)
- Look: low-poly clay/flat-shaded, a Dalí homage (motifs only, never a replica). Palette: sand ochre, Cap de Creus gold cliffs, sea teal #4f8486, sky cream #f2cf96 to teal, soft-watch cream + gold rims, detective red coat, ghost white with an upturned black moustache.
- Mood and light: late afternoon, Port Lligat, August 1931. Warm sun #ffd6a0 from the left (dir -1,.42,.55), long shadows, hemi #cfe3e0/#8a5a34, fog #e8cf9e 40-170, PMREM env from the sky. The setting sun is the room timer (G.DAY 240 s): dusk() lerps to night and the watches melt more.
- Camera: third-person follow, fov 62 portrait / 48 landscape, D 10.5 H 7.4 portrait, 8.6/5.4 landscape, looks 2.2 m ahead; drag to turn.
- Hero: the detective (red coat, black hat, lantern); the ghost is a white sheet with a curled moustache, rises out of hiding and flies.
- World: foreground velvet ropes + the door back to the hall; midground the 8 searchable things (olive tree with a watch, block, ants watch, the sleeper with the saddle watch, crutch-propped soft shape, café table with Camembert, an empty gold frame, the Port Lligat boat); background the sea, gold cliffs, headlands, a mesa.
- Budget: ~67-73 draw calls, ~43-44k triangles (phone fine). Shadows 1024 phone / 2048 desktop.

## the hall (v2.1, Marci's design)
- A curved terracotta apse (RAD 8.2, CZ 1.5, arc +-1.22 rad) on a cream/teal checker floor that runs to the horizon; eggs on the wall top and bread rolls on the inner face (Figueres museum nods, motifs only); the left wall end has gone soft and leans on a giant crutch; a long moustached shadow with nobody casting it; a far elephant on very long legs walks along the horizon; a velvet bench with a soft watch draped over its end.
- Centre: the empty glass case under a light cone (SpotLight `spot`, intensity per room via def.spotI so the shader never recompiles), red velvet slab with the dust outline, placard "L'APOCALYPSE DE SAINT JEAN / its bronze cover lay here / STOLEN". Pieces won back float in the case as gold coins (R.coins).
- Three gilded frames are the doors, left to right: 1904 Two Salvadors (wet paint, soon), 1931 The Soft Hour (the desert, open), 1926 The Academy (open since v2.3). Paintings are canvas homages (paintTwins: two identical drops, one reflects a sun and one a moon; paintSoft; paintAcademy: checker floor, broken column, easel with an empty canvas, plaster head with a drawer). Soon doors: darker, the paint slides down (UV melt over ~80 s), drips run off the bottom bar into a puddle; tapping one wobbles it and the ghost says it's still drying. Lit doors: lamp on, warm halo on the wall, wash over the painting, a star rating on the door button.
- Camera: hallView() portrait (0,7.2,12.6) -> (0,.9,-3.4), landscape (0,5,10.5) -> (0,.7,-3.4), tuned so the case top projects below the door bottoms (scratchpad proj.py does the maths); drag turns +-.45 about (0,0,-1), slow sway unless reduced motion.

## room 1904, Two Salvadors (v2.2)
- Puzzle: spot the difference in a mirror. A huge gold-framed mirror (MIR OW 16 x OH 4.6 opening in a plum wall, WT 5.6) splits a dusk room; on this side 8 things on a tiled dusk-blue floor, in the mirror their twins. 3 random things (G.chain) differ in the mirror. Tap a thing or its reflection: a right one = gold ring on both sides + a true 1904 fact; a wrong one = a fork ("Identical. Like two drops of water."). 3 found -> the ghost rises (the same chase as the desert), caught = the 1904 gem piece. Hide-again falls back to the riddle chain (CLUES.brother q).
- Things (x, z): horse -5/4.9 (red, twin teal), lamp -3.6/4.1 (twin dark), egg -2.15/5 (twin cup empty), drop -.7/4.1 (sun inside, twin moon), clock .7/5 (two faces, twin a different hour), chair 2.15/4.1 (ochre, twin turned 90 deg), cypress 3.6/5 (twin leans the other way), shoes 5/4.1 (twin missing one).
- Facts used (FACTS1904): born in Figueres, Catalonia, 1904; older brother also named Salvador died 1903, before he was born; the Theatre-Museum opened in Figueres in 1974. Nothing else stated as fact.
- Losing recipe: "Consommé for Two, served in a mirror" (our own).
- How the mirror works: group MW with scale.z -1 holds clones of each thing (shared materials) + a twin detective + twin ghost synced in R.tick; differences only touch the twin (material swap by material.name, hide a part, rotate a part; DIFF map in buildBrother). The wall doesn't cast shadows (mirror-world shadow artifacts). def.kind 'diff': newGame calls R.setDiffs(G.chain), search() goes to diffSearch() until G.hid. Probes: scratchpad hm/dgh b1 (look), b2 (full play: alias tap, wrong, found, chase, win, hall relit, cooked; needs dgh/hlong.sh, CDPT 430), b3 (memory over 3 visits).
- Camera: ROOMS.brother.cam {D [13.5, 8.4], H [8.6, 4.9], A 4, Y 1.1, fov [72, 48]} via followCfg() and ROOMFOV; all eight things and their reflections fit at 390x844.

## room 1926, The Academy (v2.3)
- Madrid's drawing studio on exam day, made surreal: checker floor to the horizon, a colonnade standing alone (one column broken, its beam gone soft), a big canvas on a studio easel, one lamp on a tripod, three plaster casts on brass turntables (left moustache curl, a bust, right curl), a blackboard ("EXAMEN · 1926", a cube, a sphere, a moustache doodle, "¿Quién juzga a quién?") with a soft watch over its top, the examiners' table with three empty high-backed chairs melting at different rates, a plaster head with a drawer, a student's easel with a blank canvas.
- Puzzle (def.kind 'shadow'): the lamp (point light at SHD.L (0,1,2.6)) throws the casts' shadows onto the canvas (plane z SHD.ZC -4.4, 5 x 3.3 m). Each cast turns in 45 deg steps (tap it, its button in #turns, keys 1/2/3, Shift = back, or drag it: free spin, snaps on release). The chalk line is the target: a bust with a huge upturned moustache. Correct steps: curls {0}, bust {0,4}. Starts: curls 2..6, bust 1,2,3,5,6,7. A cast at its step locks (chime, gold, a FACTS1926 fact). Each cast has its own stretch of chalk line: pale chalk -> gold as it gets close (dmax 4 curls, 2 bust), locked = gold pulse. Hints: 30 s general, 60 s the furthest cast, then every 30 s that cast's taps (or "drag it back" when > 4). No forks are lost during the puzzle; walking to things only looks (R.look).
- All three in place: two eyes open in the shadow, "Judge me? Nobody in this academy is competent to judge me!", the ghost peels off the canvas (dark, flat -> white, round) and the usual chase starts (R.ghostBox keeps him in the studio). Hide-again uses CLUES.academy riddles; the canvas thing is nohide; heat distances scaled by R.heatS .45 (the room is small); the camera switches to follow once he has hidden.
- How the shadow works: each cast is a list of convex parts (ell/cap/cyl) in its own frame; castPolys() projects each part's point cloud (Fibonacci sphere, 62 pts) through the lamp onto the canvas plane and takes its 2D hull; all hulls fill as one path (nonzero = clean union) into a 1024x676 canvas texture (STEX), repainted only when dirty (~3 ms). The base layer (cloth, lamp pool, the stands' faint pole shadows) is cached. The detective throws a shadow too when he walks into the beam (MEP parts). The curls are designed in shadow space (CURL points) and moved back to the cast at z .2 (scale 7/2.4). Chalk: per cast a plane in front of the canvas; ring = dilate(union, 15) minus union (dark) + dilate 11 minus dilate 4 (chalk, grainy), masked to near that cast's own correct shadow. Canvas gotcha: destination-in applies per draw call, so stroke() then fill() keeps only their intersection; draw the mask in its own canvas and drawImage it once.
- Camera: CAM.mode 'stage' (R.view: portrait (0,3.6,7.4)->(0,1.3,-2) fov 64, landscape (0,3.5,9)->(0,1.6,-1.6) fov 44, drag yaw +-.45 about (0,0,-1.5)); snapFollow() snaps to it. The global spot moves per room (def.spotP/spotT/spotA, SPOT0 = the hall's).
- Facts used (FACTS1926): moved to Madrid in 1922 to study at the Royal Academy of Fine Arts of San Fernando; lived at the Residencia de Estudiantes, friends with Lorca and Buñuel; expelled in 1926 after declaring none of the professors competent to judge him. Losing recipe: "Detective in Plaster Aspic, Academy style" (our own).
- Probes: scratchpad hm/dg4 a1 (look), a2 (2 locked + 1 close), a4 (reveal moment), a5 (full play: look, button, keys, drag, solve, chase, tap-catch, won, hall lit; wait up to 70 s per step, headless runs ~3 fps), a6 (hints), a7 (cooked, play again, back to the brother room).

## the finale in the hall (v2.4, Marci's design)
- Wakes when all three doors are lit (finPending(): litCount() === 3 and not SAVE.fin, or a replay via FINRE.on). buildFinale(R) only runs then, so a fresh hall is unchanged. Stages in R.fin.stage: 'sec' -> 'melt' -> 'talk' -> 'egg' -> 'hatch' -> 'done' (G.state 'fin' during the cutscene).
- 1) Put the museum to sleep (Marci: "disable the cameras and alarms", made surreal): two floating eyes on crutches above the doors (x +-1.53; blink cycle 5.6 s, dozing from 3.1 s, lashes flutter just before they wake; awake eyes follow the detective) and two lobster telephones on chests of drawers beside the case (x +-1.62, ring every 2.1 s). Tap one / its #sens button / keys 1-4: the detective walks there; a dozing eye closes, a phone is lifted. An eye that is awake when you arrive stares, a red flash, it wakes one random sleeper and steps you back (never a fail). Dream logic only, no real security advice.
- 2) The case melts like a soft watch (meltGeo on the segmented glass + edges: slump, spread, drape over the velvet, drip off the plinth edges); nothing is stolen, the case is empty. Coins orbit, the ghost rises and confesses in 4 original lines (FINLINES, Go on ›), shrinks away and leaves an egg; tap it (or Crack the egg / Enter) -> it shakes, cracks (zigzag halves, gold inside), a spiral staircase (56 checker steps, gold rail) unfurls up into soft light. End card #fin: "The egg hatched", "The dream floor opens soon." + a tease (lobster telephone, lips sofa, elephants on stilts, drawers), Back to the hall / Share.
- Saved as SAVE.fin = 1 (dalis-ghost:hall); a returning player sees the melted case, the empty egg and the staircase; the ↺ door button (or key 4) dreams the ending again (FINRE.on, the save stays).
- Camera: finView('case') portrait (0,3.1,6.9)->(0,1.45,.2), landscape (0,2.9,6.4)->(0,1.85,.2); finView('stair') portrait (0,2.6,14.5)->(0,7.6,.2), landscape (0,2.6,16.5)->(0,7.4,.2). The detective watches from (1.4,0,1.8) in portrait, (2.6,0,1.5) in landscape (else he covers the right phone). hallPath's side waypoint is 2.45 when R.fin exists (walks around the phones).
- Budget: the finale hall is ~200 draw calls / ~44k triangles (was ~70); fine on phones in tests but the first place to trim (merge the eyes/phones) if Marci reports lag.
- Probes: scratchpad hm/dg5 f1 (wake), f2 (puzzle -> confession), f3 (egg, stairs, card, save; headless needs ~200 s), f4 (done state on load), f5/f5t (replay, landscape talk framing; set F.mk = .97 to skip the melt), f6 (keyboard only: 4 replays, 1-4, Enter through talk + egg), f7 (Back to the hall), f8 (tap target sizes: .sen 80x48). Headless gotchas: a long walk at ~3 fps lets an eye wake up (by design); __DG has no hallUI.

Hard rules: homage, never a replica of real artworks (motifs and moods only: soft watches, crutches, drawers, ants, eggs, long shadows); facts must be accurate (only ones I'm sure of or verified); no external links; no database.
