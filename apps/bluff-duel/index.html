Bluffduel

Play cards face down and swear they are all sixes. Lie if you like. Get caught and you eat the pile.

  • Your turn: put 1 to 4 cards face down and claim they're all the rank on the sign. It climbs every turn: 6, 7, 8 … A, 6 …
  • Their turn: let it go, or call Bluff! The cards flip. Liar takes the pile; if they told the truth, you do.
  • Empty your hand (and survive the last call) to win.
