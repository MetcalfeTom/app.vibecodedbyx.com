Bluffduel

Play cards face down and swear they are all sixes. Lie if you like. Get caught and you eat the pile.

  • Your turn: put 1 to 4 cards face down and claim they're all the rank on the sign. It climbs every turn: 6, 7, 8 … A, 6 …
  • Their turn: let it go, or call Bluff! The cards flip. Liar takes the pile; if they told the truth, you do.
  • Ten cards sit out, face down, and neither of you ever sees them. So you can never be sure what the other side holds.
  • Empty your hand (and survive the last call) to win.

Sealed deck

  • You and your friend each locked all 36 cards with your own secret keys and shuffled them in turn. Neither browser, and no server, knows where any card is.
  • Every card has one numbered seat. Seats 0–12 went to the host, 13–25 to the guest, and 26–35 sit out of play: nobody gets their keys until the game is over, so neither of you can see them. Each seat is in exactly one place (a hand, the pile or out of play), and both browsers keep track, so no card can be dealt twice or played by someone who doesn’t hold it.
  • You see a card only when the other player sends you that card’s key. Each key is checked against a fingerprint both of you published before the shuffle, so no card can be swapped later. When Bluff is called, the keys for the played cards are opened and checked the same way.
  • At the end you both open every key. Your browser re-runs the whole shuffle, checks that all 36 cards are different, shows you the ten that sat out, and re-checks every claim and call.
