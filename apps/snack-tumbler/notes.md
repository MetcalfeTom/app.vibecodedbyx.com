## issues
- LESSON (+): a `position:fixed; inset:0` CANVAS does not stretch — replaced elements keep their intrinsic 300×150 unless width/height are set. The probe passed (scene existed) while the screenshot showed a postage stamp; screenshot pass caught it.
- Camera-relative movement: the initial orbit angle must put the camera BEHIND the rat (theta 0 here) or "forward" walks away from the dish — the probe caught theta=π.
- Keyboard Space: don't gate on event.target being body/canvas (probes and some browsers target window); gate only on BUTTON focus so a focused button keeps its own Space behaviour.
- Probes that tap a projected snack must clear the table first — a nearer snack occludes the raycast; and at 320/390 a snack at (-3,-2) is off-screen.
- Probe ordering: chew right after an auto-grab is refused (grab anim .35 s) — step ~25 frames first. Listener audit = wrap EventTarget.prototype.addEventListener BEFORE the app script (injected at <head> by run.sh), snapshot counts, drive input, compare.
- Tone mapping bleach: ACES at exposure 1.0 + hemi .72 + key 1.25 turned the clay palette white (rat read as a snowman). Exposure .88 / hemi .55 / key 1.0 and a darker rat grey fixed it — screenshot pass, not probes.
- Auto-follow probe: W always walks AWAY from the camera, so "camera eases back behind the rat" is vacuous with W — strafe with D to test follow.
- Headless WebGL works here (swiftshader) with the plain `--disable-gpu` flags; screenshots need `--virtual-time-budget` (rAF loop).
