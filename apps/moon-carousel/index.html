The carousel

The Moon is drawn the way it hangs in your sky: lit side toward the Sun, turned for your latitude, rocking slightly as its face wobbles. Its size is exaggerated so you can see it; its place above the horizon is real (compass along the bottom). Rise and set are when the Moon's top edge meets a flat horizon.
