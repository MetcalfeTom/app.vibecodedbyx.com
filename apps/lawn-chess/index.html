Garden rules

Plain chess on a sunny lawn, against the computer or a friend on the same screen. Tap a piece, then tap where it goes.

Spoiler: something lives under this lawn

A mole. Every few turns it pops up on an empty square, usually a busy one. While it's up, no piece can land on that square or pass through it, and nobody can capture it. A few turns later it burrows away again.
