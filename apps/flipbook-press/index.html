    How to make the flipbook
    1. Print at 100% (actual size), no fit to page.
    2. Cut along the dashed lines. Thicker paper flicks better.
    3. Stack them in number order, the cover on top, then frame 1, lined up on the numbered tab.
    4. Staple or clip the tab end twice, then bend the pages and let them slip past your thumb.
