repair lens

an AR repair assistant that knows what it doesn't know: simulated feed (your camera stays off), identification with honest uncertainty, hazard gates before a single screw turns, and guidance that stops at the safe boundary — everything past it says "professional".

PROTOTYPE · the camera preview is a simulation — this page never requests camera access. real computer vision would need on-device models this page deliberately does not ship. diagnostic confidence figures are illustrative of the design, not measurements.
