How it squishes

Every block carries the weight of everything stacked above it. Soft blocks squish under that weight, solid ones don't. Use all the blocks and land the top of the tower inside the pink band.

Wide floors: two blocks stand side by side on each floor, with a board on top. A block carries more of the weight above when that weight sits closer to it, so moving things sideways changes what squishes. Drag a stacked block left or right to slide it along its floor. Use every block and keep every board level.
