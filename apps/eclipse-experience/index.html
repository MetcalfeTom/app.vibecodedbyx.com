eye safety — non-negotiable

  • Never look at the sun without ISO 12312-2 eclipse glasses — sunglasses do nothing, camera sensors and eyes burn the same way.
  • Glasses stay ON for every partial phase. Only inside totality itself — moon fully covering the sun — is it safe to look with bare eyes, and only until the first sliver returns.
  • No glasses? A pinhole projector (two sheets of paper, one with a small hole) projects the crescent safely onto the ground.
  • Binoculars/telescopes need dedicated front-mounted solar filters — eclipse glasses behind optics are not enough.
