# Eight Count

A choreography tool for dance instructors: load a song, the beat is found, the song splits into eight-counts, and each count gets a move. A 3D toon dancer performs the routine on the beat. Asked for by pushedbutton (Twitch) on 2026-10-05, inspired by Harmony's 8-count window: "a 3D dancer with editable moves, beat matched to a song with a wave form. No need for a crate or playlist".

## log
- v1.9 (2026-10-05): wrists and ankles (Pushed, after making 'Look at my Shoe'). New pose keys lwb/rwb (wrist bend, + curls toward the palm), lwt/rwt (wrist turn about the forearm, + turns the palm forward), la/ra (ankle, + points the toes); all 0 in N(), swapped by mirror. The hand now hangs from a wrist group at the forearm's end (arm() returns [sh, el, wr, hd]; the rainbow trace and DANCER.hand() use hd). Planted feet keep the sole flat, then the ankle turns on top (toes can dip into the floor; a real tiptoe would need the stance spot raised). Built-in moves leave them at 0. Probe hm/pec12.js.
- v1.8 (2026-10-05): move maker, step 1 of Pushed's move editor. '＋ make a move' in the palette (or '✎ edit move' on a custom slot) opens a maker: the stage sticks to the top, the routine editor hides. A move is keys on a 0..1 timeline (starts with a start and an end key, per Pushed: 'start and end keyframe, tweening, add a frame between if required'); keyPose() eases between keys with smoothstep. Joint sliders with real limits (JG table: arms forward/out/elbow, legs forward/out/knee, spine sway/lean/tilt/twist, head, turn, x/y in cm); moving a slider auto-keys at the scrub point (scrub snaps to a key within .025). Add / delete key, mirror (swap sides), loop preview, name, length 1-16 counts, 'start from' any built-in (sampled into 5 or 9 keys). Saved in localStorage `eight-count-moves` (cleanMove validates ids /^c[a-z0-9]{2,16}$/ and at most 64 keys); a deleted move's slots become bounce, and restore() maps unknown ids to bounce. Probes hm/pec10.js (flow), pec11.js (phone).
- v1.7 (2026-10-05): floor marks (Pushed: 'any little fun embellishments'): a turn draws an amber ring round her feet (RingGeometry drawRange by progress) that fades over the next count; a jump landing sends one white ripple out over the following count. floorAt() in the app script, floorMarks() in the module; DANCER.marks() for probes (hm/pec9.js). Ideas: stomp ripple, sparkle on 'hit a pose'.
- v1.6 (2026-10-05): practice speed (100/75/50%, saved in prefs as rate): song time t plays at audio time startAt + t / RATE via playbackRate (pitch drops like a slowed record; a pitch-keeping version would need a media element or a time-stretch). Count-in, click and the editor preview all scale. The dancer is named Emma, after the dance teacher who inspired Pushed. Probe hm/pec8.js measured 0.75 / 0.50.
- v1.5 (2026-10-05): faster / slower for every move (Pushed's half-time / double-time idea). A slot holds id or id*L (L counts, from LENS .5-32; ½ = twice in one count); sl() parses, slotAt() is the raw slot, moveAt() the id, runIdx() compares raw slots, progAt() gives the move's progress. relen() rewrites the run from its start; counts it gives up become holds. Probe hm/pec6.js / pec7.js (phone, scrolled to the editor).
- v1.4 (2026-10-05): the rainbow, Pushed's first long move: 16 counts. Moves can now have a length (MOVES 5th field); runIdx() counts the same move right before a count, so 16 rainbows in a row = one rainbow, 8 = half. The right hand paints up to the top, both hands meet overhead, the left hand carries it down; a 7-band trail (traced from the hand path, projected onto a clean arc) appears behind the fingertips and fades over 4 counts after. Picking a long move fills its counts, spilling into the next eights. Demo groove has two eights of rainbow (eights 5-6). Probe hm/pec5.js (__EC.t0 freezes the preview clock).
- v1.3 (2026-10-05): gravity, for Pushed. Legs use two-bone IK so a foot stays planted on the floor unless the move lifts it (lift = how much higher than the lowest foot, plus a jump). Hip sway test: both feet stayed at y 0.035-0.048 and didn't slide; the kick still lifted the right foot to 0.63. DANCER.feet() returns world foot positions for probes (hm/pec4.js).
- v1 (2026-10-05): three.js r170 by full URL (no import map). Toon dancer built from capsules (hips, spine, head, arms with elbows, legs with knees; the hips drop so the standing foot stays on the floor). 16 moves as pose functions of the beat phase, blended from the previous beat's end pose over the first 30% of each beat; "hold" repeats the last pose. Demo groove synthesized at 112 bpm (16 eights) with a showy demo routine. Load a song: decodeAudioData, then a tempo guess (bass-weighted onset envelope at 100 fps, comb over 70-180 bpm with a soft 118 bpm prior). Timing drawer: ÷2 ×2, tap tempo (sets the phase while playing), ±10 ms nudge, "move the 1". Waveform shows four eights around the one you edit plus a whole-song strip; tap to jump. Editor: 8 slots, tap a count then a move (auto-advances), copy to the next eight, repeat to the end, all bounce. Optional click track (accent on the 1). Saved per song (file name + rounded duration) in localStorage `eight-count-v1`. Paused: the dancer loops the eight you're editing.
- v1.1 (2026-10-05, pushedbutton's first adjustments): "mark the 1" button (and key 1), like Harmony: the beat at the playhead becomes count 1, snapping to the nearest beat within 120 ms, otherwise the grid moves to the press; offset is kept as a phase and `one` as the beat index mod 8, so at most 7 beats of intro. Port and starboard colours: her left sleeve, hand back and trouser leg red, her right green, with a legend on the stage. Two-tone glove hands: coloured back, white palm facing her body when the arms hang (two hemispheres split on x, scaled flat).
- v1.2 (2026-10-05, pushedbutton): count-in toggle "5 6 7 8": play starts four beats before the 1 of the eight you're on (pre-roll silence via src.start(when) when that's before the song starts, so now() can be negative), a 660 Hz blip on each count-in beat and speechSynthesis saying five/six/seven/eight (best effort, timing depends on the browser's voice). Seeks while playing skip the count-in (play(true)). Counts light up in the intro too; the stage says "count in" for the last four beats before the 1. View button cycles front / back (camera turned round, her left is your left) / mirror (root.scale.x = -1, red arm on your left like a reflection). Click, count-in and view are remembered in localStorage `eight-count-prefs`.

## issues
- Tempo guess can land on half or double time, and the "1" can sit on the wrong beat; the timing drawer fixes both. Downbeat detection isn't attempted.
- Songs themselves are not stored (too big); load the same file again to get its routine back.
