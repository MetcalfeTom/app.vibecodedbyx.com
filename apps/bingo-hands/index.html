    About the signs: hand shapes follow common BSL finger counting, drawn as you see them, with the back of the hand towards you (10 is the one that shows the palm). Signs vary a lot by region. Here 11 to 19 are the unit sign waggled side to side (13 = 3, waggled) and 20 to 90 are signed digit by digit (23 = 2, then 3).
