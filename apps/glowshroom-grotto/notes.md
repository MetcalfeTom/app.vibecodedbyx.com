## issues
- Shroom/platform positions are hand-authored in 320×240 pixel units — ANY level edit must re-run the bot-search reachability sim (job-tmp `sim.js` pattern: stub DOM, `new Function(script)`, BFS over `__MG.step`), not an eyeball check. v1.0 shipped with 14/20 shrooms physically unreachable because the check was superficial.
- Max rise per hop is ~38px (apex 42.6, player must clear the ledge top). Keep tier gaps ≤38 and pit gaps ≤40 (flight ≈72px at full run).
- `fit()` must stay idempotent: `ResizeObserver` fires after the first rAF paint, and setting `canvas.width` clears the picture — an unconditional resize there shows a blank frame (headless screenshots caught it).
- Level constants are exposed on `window.__MG` (`VW/VH/WORLD/GROUND/PLATS/…`) plus `buf`, `draw`, `fit`, `scale()`, `rankFor` — keep them, the probes depend on all of them.
