Glowshroom Grotto

how to play (worth ten seconds)
  • Move: ←→ or AD · Jump: space, ↑ or W — hold longer to jump higher.
  • Eat every glowshroom — pink ones are +10, the three golden ones are +50 and live in awkward places.
  • Hazards: gloom slugs patrol and crystal spikes are pointy. Touching one is a tumble, never an ending — the last lantern brings you back, score intact.
  • Lanterns are checkpoints. Walk past to light them.
  • R restarts the whole grotto whenever you like. Clear it fast with few tumbles for a better rank.
