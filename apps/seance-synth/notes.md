## log

Visuals: fixed fx canvas ABOVE the cards (pointer-events none) with drifting fog, rising ghosts, floating letters, a lightning flash + room shake on thunder/cackle/chains, a blood pulse on heartbeat/footsteps, cobweb SVGs in two corners.
