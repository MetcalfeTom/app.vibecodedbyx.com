# Vector Rift — notes

## log
- 2026-09-24 v1.0: 3D space racer per chat (xyzfela: "create some super cool 3d game" → poll → "A sounds cool" = space racer). three.js r128. Endless seeded rift (sinusoid centre path), orange ring gates (combo ×1–8, +boost), drifting asteroids (3 shields, 1.3 s invuln), soft rift walls that bleed speed, boost meter, speed ramps with distance. Attract-mode autopilot on the title screen. WASD/arrows, drag to steer, Space/Shift boost, touch BOOST button, P pause, M mute. Local best score. 70s space-poster palette (teal-black + sodium orange, banded gas giant with rings), Syncopate + Azeret Mono.
- 2026-09-24 v2.0: full redesign per a public request ("deeper racing loop with meaningful choices, not cosmetics") after xyzfela called v1 "not a very good game". v1 had no opponent and no decisions, so v2 is a RACE: a 10 km canyon, five rival pilots (KESTREL/VANTA/ORIOLE/MAGPIE/HALCYON, shuffled into roles each race: steady, burners, one blocker), you start P6 at the back of a staggered grid, 3-2-1 countdown (you can pick a line during it). Boost has three jobs: overtake, takedown, escape. Drafting (tuck in <30 u behind a ship) charges boost and adds 5% speed, but you have almost no warning of the rocks behind it. Gates feed boost and every 5th in a row repairs a shield. Boosting into a rival from behind = TAKEDOWN (+500, spin-out) and that rival holds a GRUDGE (xyzfela voted A): red glow, REVENGE tag, hunts you and rams on boost. The blocker telegraphs (wings flash + CUT tag + double-blip) 0.6 s before cutting across. Rocks spin out rivals too. Three sectors with splits, finish standings with projected times, DAILY RIFT (UTC-date seed, same course for everyone) + random rifts, best time per rift in localStorage `vector-rift-times` (cap 40). Canyon: vertex-coloured rock rings in 160 u chunks along the path, open slit on top so the ringed gas giant stays visible, glowing mineral seams, runway lights, start gantry with countdown lamps, checkered finish arch. Every ship has wingtip trails; name tags are projected from 3D, nearest first, skipped when they would overprint a closer one (xyzfela: "you decide" → only near ships).
- Balance (bot sims over 40 seeds): 'good' pilot 0 deaths, wins ~60 %, leads within ±1.7 s. 'mid' human-like bot 7 deaths, mostly P1–P2. 'basic' mostly dies. Idle pilot never wins (asserted).

## issues
- v2 camera: a lerp on camera Z lags ~20 u at top speed and, when several engine steps run before one draw (probe, or a hitch), leaves the camera outside the canyon looking at the sky. Z is now locked to chase distance; only x/y ease, with a snap when >30 u off.
- Screenshots of overlays with entry animations come out EMPTY (opacity 0 at t=0). The shot tail injects `*{animation:none;transition:none}`; a probe checking computed opacity mid-transition reads the in-between value, so check classes instead.
- Portrait phones: fixed 70° vertical FOV made the ship fill the screen. FOV now adds (1 − aspect)·34°.
- Headless: the rAF loop starves timers under dump-dom — freeze in the tail synchronously (`__VR.freeze(true)`) and run the suite inside the load handler, no setTimeout. Stepping 900 frames with a render each is too slow in swiftshader — step the engine directly, draw once.
- 320 px: boost bar + tool buttons overlapped; narrow media query shrinks both.
