how to play

Drop cookies, place magnets to bend their paths, and dunk them in the milk glass. Dunks within 1.5 seconds of each other chain into combos. Cookies that touch a floating powerup trigger it.

  • Pointer: tap empty space = drop cookie (or place magnet with 🧲). Drag a cookie to fling it. Drag a magnet to move it, tap it to flip polarity, right-click to remove.
  • ←↑→↓ move the reticle (hold Shift for big steps)
  • Space use current tool at reticle · C drop cookie · M place magnet
  • F flip nearest magnet · Delete remove nearest magnet
  • 1/2 cookie / magnet tool · P pause · R reset · S sound

Powerups: 🧲 mega magnet · 🔃 polarity chaos · 🐌 slow-mo · ⭐ golden hour (×5) · 🌧️ cookie rain
